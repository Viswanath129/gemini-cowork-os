# Alpha Trial - Times The Metrics Lied

This document captures the divergence between our quantitative metrics and the qualitative reality of decision quality. It acts as a safeguard against Goodhart's Law (when a metric becomes a target, it stops being a good metric).

## Methodology
Every 10 missions, the lead investigator audits the `ALPHA_TEST_LOG.csv` against the `WHY_USERS_DIDNT_TRUST_US.md` and `HOW_USERS_THINK.md` reports. If a mission achieved high scores (e.g., GSR=1, JAS=1) but resulted in poor long-term decision quality or human frustration, it is recorded here as a "Metric Failure."
