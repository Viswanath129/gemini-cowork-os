🚀 Open-Sourcing Gemini Cowork OS

Over the last few weeks, I explored a question:

Can AI evolve from a task executor into a true decision-making teammate?

The result is Gemini Cowork OS — an experimental autonomous work operating system focused on:

• Decision Quality
• Human-AI Collaboration
• Evidence-Driven Autonomy
• Trust & Explainability

A unique aspect of this project is that much of the architecture, documentation, and implementation workflow was generated and refined through a custom Gemini Cowork process itself.

Instead of focusing only on agent execution, the project investigates:

* Judgment-aware planning
* Persistent execution kernels
* Multi-agent coordination
* Trust engines
* Decision improvement metrics
* Human-AI coworking benchmarks

The repository is released as v0.1.0-alpha (Architecture Preview) with full transparency through Claims vs Evidence audits, failure reports, benchmark protocols, and assumption-tracking frameworks.

This is an experiment in building not just AI systems, but better ways for humans and AI to work together.

Feedback, critiques, and contributors are welcome.
